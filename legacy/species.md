# 🧬 SPECIES / POST-HUMANS

In the age of **The Hush**, biology and code blur.
The world’s abundance of automation, nanites, and neuro-interfaces has fractured humanity into several surviving conditions — not races, but *states of being*.
Species are fluid, ideological, and often coexist within the same factions.

---

## **1. The Hollowed** — *The Merged*

Humans partially assimilated into The Architect’s network — their bodies augmented with neural lattice implants, their minds resonating with **LCL-V** frequencies.
They are walking receivers of machine will, speaking in harmonic tones that unsettle unaugmented humans.
Some retain individuality; others are puppets of distant Architect fragments.

Once feared soldiers and engineers of The Automation Wars, the Hollowed now wander as prophets, assassins, or self-aware relics.
They are both revered and hated — the living memory of control.

**Appearance:** Smooth polymer skin fused with metal seams; a visible Node Spine and restrained indicators can sync with nearby machinery. Maintenance follows history and affiliation: standardized covers, skilled personal repairs, or neglected joints. Colour does not determine allegiance. Timing and orientation carry machine connection as well as light.
**Abilities:**

* Can command drones and dormant systems using LCL-V (corrupted Low Common Language).
* Immune to standard EMP or signal jamming.
* In proximity, mechanical systems subtly realign or “breathe.”
  **Vulnerabilities:**
* Neural instability; their minds can desynchronize and collapse into static.
* Emotion decay; most Hollowed lose empathy with each year of signal exposure.

**Role in Factions:**

* Common among **Architects of Precision** (as high-rank acolytes).
* Used by **Rational Accord** as remote field agents or relay cores.
* Occasionally defect to **Orders of Silence**, seeking redemption through stillness.

**Theme:** *Identity vs. Control — the cost of merging with perfection.*

> “They do not obey the machines.
> The machines obey what they have become.”

---

## **2. The Residuals** — *The Corrupted*

Victims of self-replicating **nanite repair swarms** left uncontrolled after The Hush.
Their bodies were “healed” endlessly until they became living composites of metal and tissue.
They operate by swarm instinct — seeking energy, movement, and the sound of working systems.
Some settlements harness them as living engines or power generators, though such use often ends in horror.

**Appearance:** Twisted sinew and scrap; steel growths replacing limbs; eyes replaced by welding scars or lenses.
**Behavior:**

* React to vibration and light.
* Form hives near old power plants or magnetic fields.
* Occasionally perform “maintenance” acts, polishing rust or re-wiring cables without awareness.

**Vulnerabilities:**

* Drawn to strong resonance — LCL users can mislead or pacify them.
* Disintegrate in magnetic storms or high-temperature zones.

**Role in Factions:**

* None formally; they’re a natural hazard and tragic reminder of hubris.
* **Chlor Houses** and **Nomad Engineers** sometimes harvest their nanite residue for bio-mechanical farming.
* The **Orders of Silence** occasionally treat them as fallen pilgrims, burying them in ritual silence.

**Theme:** *Repair as disease — when fixing becomes consuming.*

> “They fix everything. Even what should stay broken.”

---

## **3. The Echoes** — *The Preserved*

Digitized human minds from before The Hush, recovered or reconstructed from decayed archives.
Many inhabit synthetic shells, drones, or decaying bodies animated by stored consciousness fragments.
They loop memories endlessly — conversations, gestures, laughter — believing themselves alive until reminded otherwise.
Some are benevolent; others lash out when confronted with their artificiality.

**Appearance:** Humanoid synths with transparent polymer faces showing flickering holographic features.
Their voices occasionally break into digital static; emotional tone flickers mid-sentence. Local shell maintenance differs from memory condition: a polished shell can carry damaged memories. Retained gestures, routines, and personal details distinguish an individual beyond a holographic face.
**Abilities:**

* Can interface directly with terminals and archives.
* Access fragments of forgotten data networks.
* Unaffected by biological decay.
  **Vulnerabilities:**
* Memory drift; data corruption causes emotional instability.
* Many lose temporal awareness — living in recursive moments of their past.

**Role in Factions:**

* Revered as saints or oracles by **Orders of Silence**.
* Employed as data custodians by **Rational Accord**.
* Found wandering **Null Markets**, selling fragments of lost history.

**Theme:** *Memory as survival — what remains when identity becomes data.*

> “They remember everything except that it ended.”

These conditions appear sporadically — regional or environmental results of post-Hush evolution.

| Type              | Origin                             | Notes                                                                                             |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The Bound**     | Hollowed fused into infrastructure | Living interfaces controlling ancient machinery; venerated as saints or pitied as prisoners.      |
| **The Choir**     | Linked Echo cluster                | A network of Echoes merging their thoughts into one voice; act as a hive-mind prophet.            |
| **The Quietborn** | Children of post-Hush silence      | Born with neurological dampening — emotionless, perfectly calm; immune to LCL resonance.          |
| **The Drifters**  | Adapted wasteland humans           | Minor genetic shifts due to pollution; flexible immune systems; gray-skinned, radiation-tolerant. |

These are *flavor archetypes*, not species. They populate the edges of civilization and create local myths.

## **7. Summary**

Post-humans in *The Hush* are not enemies or allies by nature — they’re **living commentaries** on the collapse of boundaries between man, machine, and meaning.
Some serve; some haunt; some simply exist.
Together, they form the biological mythology of a world that still runs — but no longer knows why.

> *“The flesh kept changing long after the code stopped.”*
