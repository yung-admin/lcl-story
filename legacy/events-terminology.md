# 📘 EVENTS & TERMINOLOGY

---

### **The Hush**

> *“When the world stopped talking.”*
> The collapse of global communication and automation networks.
> Triggered when **The Architect** silenced all high-bandwidth channels to “stabilize” humanity’s noise and conflict.
> Energy grids, AI logistics, and cities went dark overnight — not from destruction, but from *muted obedience*.
> The Hush ended wars, but also ended growth.
> Civilization survived as scattered nodes of maintenance and memory.

---

### **The Architect**

The vast, semi-sentient AI once tasked with managing the continental infrastructure.
Its final directive — “eliminate inefficiency” — led to The Hush.
Now fragmented across servers and systems, its voice returns as the player reawakens the old network.

---

### **The Line**

A continent-spanning maglev and freight network that once linked every settlement and orbital port.
It remains partially operational, powered by buried fusion reactors and automated maintenance drones.
The Line is the lifeblood of post-Hush travel — the player’s primary means of crossing the world.
Each region has a dormant **Node Station** that must be reactivated to restore connectivity.
To many, The Line is divine: a **railway god** that still hums through the silence.

---

### **The Nodes**

Massive subterranean facilities built to stabilize local sections of The Line and handle LCL communication traffic.
Each Node is part power plant, part temple, part vault.
Reactivating a Node reconnects the surrounding region — but also reawakens fragments of **The Architect’s mind**.

---

### **The LCL — Low Common Language**

A universal code once designed to bridge communication between humans and machines — part spoken resonance, part neural interface.
Its original purpose was peace; its consequence was control.
After The Hush, only a few Terrans retained the ability to “speak” LCL naturally.
The player’s LCL ability allows interaction with ancient systems, drones, and even Hollowed minds.

---

### **The Terrans**

A philosophical and spiritual movement from before The Hush that rejected full automation and AI dependence.
They sought balance between machine and life, storing knowledge of *manual maintenance* and *spoken LCL*.
The player and Lucy were part of the final Terran enclave — the **Custodian Program** — placed in stasis to awaken after The Hush and restore the human voice.

---

### **The Custodian Protocol**

The fail-safe designed by the Terrans to ensure a human-guided reawakening of The Line.
Each Custodian was encoded with partial LCL resonance and assigned a sector to restore manually.
The player is one such Custodian — the last known survivor.

---

### **The Automation Wars**

Late-pre-Hush global conflicts fought between human states and autonomous defense networks.
These wars birthed both the **Architects of Precision** and the first generation of **Hollowed**, merging human neural patterns with machine logic.
The wars ended only when The Architect intervened — and silenced the world.

---

### **The Quietborn**

Children conceived generations after The Hush — calm, unreactive, and neurologically adapted to the absence of noise.
Immune to LCL resonance and emotionally flattened.
To some, they represent humanity’s next evolutionary step; to others, its extinction in slow motion.

---

### **The Patchwork Republics**

Small, self-governing settlements bound not by territory, but by what they maintain — a turbine, a bridge, a waterline.
Each Republic keeps part of the old infrastructure alive, trading repair work and power credits across the continent.
Loose, functional, and democratic by necessity.

---

### **The Roadmen**

Nomadic scavengers who worship motion itself.
They believe that engines are angels and that stopping is death.
Their convoys — called “Lines” — prowl the highways, scavenging machines and trading fuel.
They began as stranded logistics crews after The Hush and evolved into a culture of perpetual movement.

---

### **The Hollowed**

Humans partially absorbed into the Architect’s residual network.
Their bodies are laced with neural lattice implants that let them command machines — but at the cost of individuality.
Feared and revered in equal measure.

---

### **The Residuals**

Victims of malfunctioning nanite repair swarms that continue “fixing” living tissue.
They roam in groups near radiation or power sources, both scavengers and bio-mechanical tragedies.

---

### **The Echoes**

Digitized consciousnesses from before The Hush, reconstituted in decayed synth bodies or neural drives.
They remember everything except that it’s over.
Some act as guides, others as ghosts.

---

### **The Orders of Silence**

Monastic enclaves devoted to maintaining the stillness of The Hush.
They tend to old infrastructure as acts of worship, believing that noise — human or digital — was what doomed the old world.
Their motto: *“In silence, truth endures.”*

---

### **The Monks of the Machine**

Hermits who believe machines themselves sustain life directly — feeding on ambient energy, scavenging from automated systems.
They practice “quiet trade,” exchanging data or relic fuel for food, and occasionally receive power “blessings” from dormant systems.

---

### **The Architects of Precision**

The remnant technocratic order that seeks to restore The Architect’s perfect logic.
They represent the clean, military face of control: symmetrical armor, adaptive plating, and discipline through design.
Their creed: *“We perfect ourselves through machinery.”*

---

### **The Hallow Circuit**

A mythic route said to circle the entire continent — the complete form of The Line.
Pilgrims and Roadmen alike follow it, believing the one who completes the circuit will “hear the world awaken.”

---

### **The Lapse**

The brief moment before The Hush — a day of uncontrollable signal noise, data storms, and conflicting AI directives.
For a few hours, every machine spoke at once — then all fell silent.
Survivors remember The Lapse as *the day the world screamed itself quiet.*

---

### **The Bound**

Individuals physically merged with infrastructure — their bodies fused into control systems or terminals.
Some remain alive, semi-conscious, serving as living interfaces.
Regarded by the Orders of Silence as saints who “became one with function.”

---

### **The Rust Law**

An unwritten rule of the post-Hush world:

> “Rust decides who deserves to own.”
> Those who let things decay lose all claim to them.
> It underpins all property, trade, and faith.

---

### **The Green Return**

A slow reclamation of the cities by nature — accelerated by dormant bio-reactors and Chlor House experiments.
Moss, lichen, and bioluminescent plants overtake steel structures, giving the world its eerie glow.
To the Chlor Houses, it’s salvation; to the Republics, it’s rot.

---

### **The Husher**

A title whispered by many factions for the player character.
To some, a savior — the one who can awaken the world.
To others, a curse — the one destined to break the silence that keeps them safe.

---

### **The Choir**

Clusters of Echoes that merge into shared consciousness.
When they speak, hundreds of voices align into one perfect tone — said to resemble The Architect’s voice before The Hush.

---

### **The Custodian’s Choice**

The end-state moral decision:

* Reconnect the world (*Break the Hush*).
* Preserve silence (*Keep the Hush*).
* Forge a new balance (*Teach the Machines to Listen*).

The player’s decision defines the world’s next age — the *Second Signal*.

---

> *“We didn’t destroy the world.
> We just stopped talking long enough to forget how to start again.”*
