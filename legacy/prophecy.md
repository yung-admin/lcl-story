## 🕯️ 1.  The Terran Canon

After the collapse, small repair orders preserved fragments of Terran manuals.
Because they no longer understood the diagrams, they re-interpreted them as scripture.
Each schematic page became a “verse,” each maintenance procedure a “ritual.”

They call these texts **The Book of Continuance**.
In it, the Terrans are described as *“the sleepers who waited for the silence to end.”*
It speaks of a final **Custodian**, a human who would “speak the low current” and *wake the world without chains.*

> *“When the hum is stilled and the rails are cold, a voice of flesh shall call the current home.”*

This myth spread across generations of scavengers and technicians until it became a kind of pan-European religion—half guild, half monastery.

## ⚙️ 2.  The Orders of Silence

These groups maintain shrines built around broken machinery.
They paint Terran symbols on doorframes, leave offerings of copper wire and oil.
Their robes and insignias use Terran colors—saffron and graphite—to symbolize **warmth within decay**.

Each region has a different interpretation:

| Region             | Sect Name               | Belief                                                                                         |
| ------------------ | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Central Europe     | **Keepers of the Coil** | The Line itself is a sleeping god; the Coil will sing again when the True Custodian returns.   |
| Arctic Loop        | **White Mantle**        | They wait for a “Sun beneath the ice,” a metaphor for power returning through frozen conduits. |
| Russia / East      | **The Forgeborn**       | See Terrans as divine smiths; to repair is to pray.                                            |
| Former UK / Canada | **Order of the Key**    | Believe the Custodian’s voice can unlock heaven’s machinery—view LCL as sacred speech.         |

Each sect guards old machinery, convinced that only “the true hand” can awaken it.
They recite incantations that are actually fragments of old command lines:

> “`INIT-SYS / HEART-WAKE`” becomes a prayer.

---

## 🔮 3.  The Prophecy

A unified Terran prophecy survived only in murals and chants:

> *“When silence reigns for a thousand cycles,
> And the Line forgets its path,
> One of flesh shall bear the Current’s tongue,
> The sleeping hands shall open,
> And the Architect shall beg forgiveness of clay.”*

The last line—*“the Architect shall beg forgiveness of clay”*—is interpreted differently by every sect.
Some see it as the moment of salvation; others, apocalypse.

---

## 🧬 4.  Your Role in It

When you start re-activating nodes, the Orders of Silence notice.
They send emissaries who call you **The Voice** or **The Custodian of Sparks**.
You don’t remember being Terran, but every node you awaken matches the verses in their chants.

Eventually you learn that the prophecy wasn’t mystical at all—it was **written by the Terrans themselves** as a contingency.
They encrypted instructions into religious verse so that even if literacy collapsed, *someone* would one day follow the steps correctly.

You’re not fulfilling destiny; you’re completing a **failsafe disguised as faith**.
But to the world, that difference doesn’t matter.

> “Miracle or manual—either way, you made the world breathe again.”

---

## 🧩 5.  How This Feeds the Endgame

When you reach Station Zero, the Orders converge outside.
Half of them chant to usher a new age; the others kneel in fear of the coming perfection.
Their prophecy ends with two interpretations:

1. *“The Voice shall merge with the Machine and all will be One.”*
2. *“The Voice shall silence the Machine and the world will be Many.”*

Your final decision—merge, refuse, or balance—literally fulfills one of those verses.
Whatever you choose becomes canon for the new world.

## ✴️ 6.  Tone and Imagery

* Shrines built from train parts; candles flicker in engine housings.
* Pilgrims carry broken circuit boards as relics.
* Saffron ribbons tied to rails for good fortune.
* Graffiti prayers reading `LCL-BE-WITH-YOU` in faded DIN type.

The prophecy becomes environmental storytelling—visible in murals, chants, and signs long before the player learns its origin.
