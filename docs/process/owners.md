# Content Owners

This document tracks ownership and responsibility for different areas of The Logos Project content.

## Primary Owners

### World Canon
- **Primary:** Worldbuilding Team
- **Backup:** Narrative Team
- **Scope:** `/world/canon/` - Core worldbuilding documents

### Factions & Societies
- **Primary:** Faction Design Team
- **Backup:** Worldbuilding Team
- **Scope:** `/world/societies/` - All faction and culture content

### Atlas & Locations
- **Primary:** Environment Design Team
- **Backup:** Worldbuilding Team
- **Scope:** `/world/atlas/` - All regional and location content

### Characters
- **Primary:** Character Design Team
- **Backup:** Narrative Team
- **Scope:** `/world/cast/` - All character content

### Narrative Arcs
- **Primary:** Narrative Team
- **Backup:** Quest Design Team
- **Scope:** `/narrative/arcs/` - Major story arcs

### Quests & Scenes
- **Primary:** Quest Design Team
- **Backup:** Narrative Team
- **Scope:** `/narrative/quests/`, `/narrative/scenes/` - Quest and scene content

### Endings
- **Primary:** Narrative Team
- **Backup:** Quest Design Team
- **Scope:** `/narrative/endings/` - All ending content

### Manuscript
- **Primary:** Manuscript Team
- **Backup:** Narrative Team
- **Scope:** `/manuscript/` - Final manuscript content

### Templates & Process
- **Primary:** Process Team
- **Backup:** All Teams
- **Scope:** `/templates/`, `/process/` - Templates and workflows

## Review Responsibilities

### Canon Review
- **Primary Reviewer:** Worldbuilding Team Lead
- **Secondary Reviewer:** Narrative Team Lead
- **Scope:** All canon documents and worldbuilding content

### Content Review
- **Primary Reviewer:** Content Team Lead
- **Secondary Reviewer:** Quality Assurance Team
- **Scope:** All narrative and gameplay content

### Technical Review
- **Primary Reviewer:** Technical Team Lead
- **Secondary Reviewer:** Process Team Lead
- **Scope:** All technical aspects, IDs, references, and structure

## Escalation Path

### Content Disputes
1. **Level 1:** Primary and Backup owners discuss
2. **Level 2:** Team leads involved
3. **Level 3:** Project lead makes final decision

### Canon Conflicts
1. **Level 1:** Worldbuilding Team Lead reviews
2. **Level 2:** All team leads discuss
3. **Level 3:** Project lead makes final decision

### Process Issues
1. **Level 1:** Process Team reviews
2. **Level 2:** All team leads discuss
3. **Level 3:** Project lead makes final decision

## Responsibilities

### Primary Owners
- Review all content in their scope
- Ensure quality and consistency
- Resolve conflicts and disputes
- Maintain documentation and standards

### Backup Owners
- Support primary owners
- Review content when primary owner unavailable
- Provide expertise and guidance
- Escalate issues when necessary

### Reviewers
- Conduct thorough reviews using established checklists
- Ensure content meets acceptance criteria
- Provide feedback and suggestions
- Approve or reject content submissions

## Change Management

### Content Changes
- All changes must be reviewed by appropriate owners
- Significant changes require approval from team leads
- Canon changes require approval from worldbuilding team lead
- Process changes require approval from process team lead

### Ownership Changes
- Ownership changes must be approved by project lead
- New owners must be trained on responsibilities
- Documentation must be updated
- Handover process must be completed
