# Aesthetic Revision Rationale

## 2026-10-03 — Selective Maintenance and Human Care

**Authorization:** the user approved the aesthetic assessment and requested its application to the docs followed by visual tests.

**Problem:** universal grime, tired machine motion, compulsory repairability, and pervasive neon-noir weakened the premise that automation still functions while excluding people. Faction practices, daylight, skilled repair, domestic beauty, and LCL recognition needed concrete direction.

**Decision:** distinguish automated upkeep, human maintenance, and neglect; scope service access; localize neon and dark comedy; make ornament and sincere care visible; differentiate faction interventions and regional construction; express LCL through attention and accommodation.

**Superseded direction:** the absolute rules that no surface looks new, all machinery is exhausted, all objects are serviceable by hand, all decoration is documentation, and every scene follows profane noir comedy. The detailed replacements are in the [visual guide](../../legacy/visual-design.md), [tone guide](../../legacy/tone-style.md), and [accepted style guide](../world/canon/style-guide.md).

**Preservation:** pre-revision copies of the 15 edited source/process documents are in [_archive/aesthetics-2026-10-03](../../_archive/aesthetics-2026-10-03/legacy/visual-design.md). The original `~/story/lcl` project remains the collection source.

**Scope:** this revises representation and tone. It does not reconcile the competing origin accounts, add mechanics, fix geography, or choose final character models. No timeline event is changed.

**Verification:** check revised rules against supporting lore, validate local links, and compare one station across three visual treatments. Concept studies stay separate from accepted character and location canon.
