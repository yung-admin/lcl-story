All six keep the principal camera/layout anchors, five people, four serving bowls/trays and readable HUD. Fine paint marks, surface wear and light boundaries vary. The shared hue family and dark mood stay close, but cream texture changes, broader amber marks and local brightness prevent perfectly isolated grain comparisons.

Rough and Tooth each received one targeted refinement after the first pass interpreted grain/tooth mainly as chips and surface wear. Both improve texture separation only partially; retained drafts and exact refinements are recorded in the provenance. No further refinement is claimed.

## Authored assessment

Shadow offers the clearest deliberate separation between tactile dark space and calm cream technology. Pigment offers the clearest integration of grain into authored opaque brushwork. Silk and Bloom are quieter variations close to the original. Rough tests a stronger tactile finish but still partly changes apparent material wear; Tooth overlaps painted texture and does not fully establish a fibrous board language. These assessments are proposals for review, not new user selections.

Judge the whole image before individual labels. In particular, compare whether grain should live across all tones, concentrate in darkness or follow the paint marks. The original Grain is available as the round reference.
