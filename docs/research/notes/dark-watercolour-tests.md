The user requested: “lets explore watercolor more, but it needs to be darker vibe.” Saved feedback marked round 01 Watercolour as **Mix**, with no trait ratings or notes. That round was not formally completed; the explicit request authorizes this focused continuation. It does not imply approval of its other styles.

## Intent

Preserve Watercolour’s material sensibility while making the mood heavier through cold daylight, restricted practical light, deeper pigment, uneasy colour and expressive brushwork. Darkness should come from art direction, with readable characters and paths. Keep the fixed trimetric station, continuous playfield, three character positions, closed gate and principal machinery.

The briefs ask for translucent washes, granulation, pooling, dry brush, paper tooth and selective ink rather than smooth modeled surfaces. Generated results can retain more source detail than requested; each study’s scene-check note records the actual outcome. Labels describe appearance, not an implemented game filter.

## User feedback and next exploration direction

On 2026-10-04, the user said: “embers give me the nicest vibe but wash is the most interesting and unique”. This is conversational feedback, not an automatically assigned website verdict or a completed round review.

- **Embers:** preferred atmosphere. Retain its umber, rust and sienna warmth, amber practical lights, and small inhabited places within the darker industrial station.
- **Wash:** preferred interest and uniqueness. Retain its broad pigment shapes, paper texture, broken contours and selective detail.

The proposed next exploration combines Embers’ atmosphere with Wash’s painting language. Test several balances: a muted warm wash, stronger amber light against charcoal shadows, and a more abstract version with large pigment shapes. Keep warmth concentrated around human activity so the overall mood remains dark. Preserve the fixed trimetric camera, character readability and station anchors; Wash’s fine layout drift is not itself an accepted design change.

These are hypotheses, not final canon. The user subsequently requested “try it”; [round 03](wash-embers-tests.md) now tests four combinations. The original round 02 generation briefs remain unchanged.

## Review focus

Use whole-image verdicts and independent trait ratings to distinguish palette, lighting, texture, filter/medium, outlines and mood. Notes can answer: does the scene feel too friendly, too decorative, too grim, too noisy or too difficult to navigate? Which painterly qualities should survive at normal gameplay size? A preference for darker mood does not settle rendering fidelity or erase small signs of care.

Before final style approval, test selected treatments in another setting and with character movement and interaction highlights. This round does not change canon, projection angles, engine, grid or gameplay rules.
