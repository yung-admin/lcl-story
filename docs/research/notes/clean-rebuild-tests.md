## Inspection and limits

Fresh Grain has clearer machine construction and calmer surfaces than the inherited chain, without dense crawling maze patterns. It uses four tall individual dispensers under a shared supply conduit. Its volumes and fine granular/mottled paint read more smoothly modeled and quieter than the original. Existing enamel chips, masonry texture and simplified hands remain; a new generation cannot guarantee zero artifacts.

Ink + Rim retains the new scene, five people, four serving bowls, palette and readable HUD. Contours sharpen coats, seams and hose folds. Cyan glints pick out piers, machine corners and some floor joints; amber edges sharpen rails and trays. Some glints extend farther than the requested short accents, and highlights/reflections read slightly brighter. Pen marks are subtle and polished rather than freely sketched. This is a useful comparison with recorded drift, not a perfectly isolated filter test.
