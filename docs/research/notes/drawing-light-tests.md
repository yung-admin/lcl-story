## Authored assessment

Nib makes pen accents readable while staying close to Grain. Rim clearly isolates a coloured-edge vocabulary, though the cyan treatment can feel too continuous. Chroma produces the clearest change in light colour and contrast. Folio is quieter and overlaps Nib. Engraved is the strongest combined drawing departure; Etch and Engraved still expose the limit of interpreting tonal marks as surface pattern. These are review observations, not a new winner.

The review can separate whether the pen itself is pleasant, whether texture contributes to shading, and whether coloured glints or richer warm light improve the world. The original Grain stays available as the selected reference.
