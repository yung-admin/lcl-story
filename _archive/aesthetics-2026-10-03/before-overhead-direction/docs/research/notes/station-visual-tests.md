# Station Visual Tests — Selective Maintenance and Recognition

## Purpose

Test the [accepted aesthetic direction](../../world/canon/style-guide.md) with one station in three treatments. These are comparative concept studies, not a chronological sequence or a final location design. Incidental character details do not establish canon.

## Fixed Scene

A European municipal rail hall, overcast daylight, an eye-level oblique view along freight rails, repeated concrete piers, overhead handling equipment, a service gallery, a distinctive pier, a passenger gate, a food outlet, a relay terminal, and passenger furnishings. Preserve architecture, camera, weather, and major anchors across edits.

## Treatments

- **A — Automated maintenance:** exact working machinery and clean contact surfaces beside neglected passenger amenities; a human waits outside a closed access gate.
- **B — Human occupation:** preserve the automated corridor, add fitted repairs, safe public access, a compact workshop and domestic corner, and ordinary personal beauty. Human maintenance has its own skill and purpose.
- **C — LCL recognition:** edit B, preserving its inhabitants and material history. A person contacts the relay; a sensor orients, a tool arm accommodates, gate access changes, and indicators coordinate with restrained effects.

## Generation

Use the built-in image generation tool, one call per asset. Generate A; use it as the inspected edit target for B; use inspected B for C. Save original prompts alongside the final project images in `images/moodboard/station-tests/`.

## Review Criteria

1. Automated care and unmet human needs coexist without universal grime.
2. Human occupation shows skilled repair, public use, domestic care, and ornament.
3. Daylight and construction establish identity independently of neon.
4. The same station remains recognizable across all three images.
5. LCL is conveyed through attention, orientation, coordination, and access.
6. Effects remain restrained and materials readable.
7. Images distinguish visual evidence from inferred motion or sound.

### A — Automated Maintenance

![Station A: automated maintenance](../../../images/moodboard/station-tests/station-a-automated-maintenance.png)

The operating freight equipment, serviced interfaces, closed gate, and worn passenger bench establish selective care. Concrete repetition and daylight supply identity without a neon wash. The still cannot prove that the freight equipment is moving or that the dispenser has rejected the person. Its wet concourse is a particular station condition, not a universal environment rule.

### B — Human Occupation

![Station B: human occupation](../../../images/moodboard/station-tests/station-b-human-occupation.png)

The camera, piers, overhead freight equipment, tracks, gate, and terminal remain closely matched to A. Repaired bench slats, embroidery, organized tools, tea, photographs, a plant, and local warm lamps make skilled work and affection visible. A fitted drain and floor intervention give the occupied corner a maintenance boundary. The gate remains closed and the freight corridor separate.

### C — LCL Recognition

![Station C: LCL recognition](../../../images/moodboard/station-tests/station-c-lcl-recognition.png)

The same inhabited station retains its structure and domestic details. The person now addresses the relay; the gate opens, the dispenser offers a cup, a crane sensor orients, and existing status indicators coordinate. Material detail remains readable without an aura or large particle effect.

### Assessment

| Criterion | Result | Limit or follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Selective care | Clear contrast between serviced freight systems and neglected passenger areas | A rejection is inferred from the closed barrier, not verified as behaviour |
| Human care | Skilled repairs, domestic routines, and ordinary beauty read clearly in B | Explore a second region before treating this workshop arrangement as universal |
| Daylight and construction | Strong identity through piers, galleries, loads, and circulation | Future studies should test a dry exterior and another daylight condition |
| Same-place comparison | Major geometry and camera remain closely preserved | Edits are visual matches, not exact engineered geometry |
| LCL recognition | C makes changed access, provision, and sensor attention visible | The hand gesture can resemble ordinary touchscreen use; rhythm and a reciprocal answer need a sequence |
| Restraint | Effects remain small and attached to equipment | Coordinated pulses and sound are not testable from a single still |

**Next useful test:** a short sequence from unanswered machine routine to recognition and accommodation, holding the person's gesture subtle. Establish a pause, orientation, coordinated intervals, and an answer. A separate regional study should test construction and daylight beyond this hall.

These images are exploratory art evidence, not final location or character canon. The scene supports the aesthetic direction; it does not verify gameplay, precise animation timing, or sound.
