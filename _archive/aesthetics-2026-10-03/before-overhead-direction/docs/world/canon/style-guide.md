# LCL / The Hush — Active Style Guide

## Authority and Scope

The user approved these aesthetic revisions on 2026-10-03. This guide is the entry point for current art direction. Its detailed sources are the revised [visual guide](../../../legacy/visual-design.md) and [tone guide](../../../legacy/tone-style.md). Pre-revision copies are [archived](../../../_archive/aesthetics-2026-10-03/legacy/visual-design.md).

Acceptance applies to aesthetics and tone. Alternative Terran, player, Lucy, and Architect origins remain unresolved; concept art does not decide them. This is a partial organization of existing material, not a completed lore migration.

## Core Rules

1. Show a working system and who it serves. Human exclusion can coexist with immaculate upkeep.
2. Distinguish automated maintenance, human care, and neglect within the same place.
3. Let repairs demonstrate skill, provenance, and decisions; tape and scrap are only part of the vocabulary.
4. Preserve European municipal and industrial construction through futuristic adaptations.
5. Give light an operator, purpose, and reason to persist; include daylight and local darkness.
6. Treat colour as an association, supported by construction, markings, behaviour, and access.
7. Make human ornament, comfort, ordinary life, and sincere care visible.
8. Differentiate factions through spatial organization and design practices.
9. Allow exact and rapid automation as well as heavy, repaired, or damaged motion.
10. Make LCL readable through attention, alignment, an answer, and accommodation; use restrained effects.
11. Place neon-noir, dark comedy, and harsher music where people and circumstances support them.
12. Scope repairability: human tools reveal service access; continental systems can exceed human comprehension.

## First Visual Test

[Station comparison brief](../../research/notes/station-visual-tests.md): generate a base station and two edits that preserve its architecture and camera. Compare automated maintenance, human occupation, and LCL recognition. Conditions are comparative, not a new chronology.

Evaluate maintenance boundaries, skilled human care, construction, functional lighting, consistent geometry, and readable machine attention. Static images cannot verify timing or sound.

## Deferred Design Decisions

Final character faces, Woy's locomotion and model, regional geography, the extent of new manufacture, and motion/audio implementation need their own decisions. Tests may explore them without locking canon.
